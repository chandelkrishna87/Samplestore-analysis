# Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Project Description

Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project based on the Sample Superstore dataset sourced from Kaggle. The project aims to evaluate retail business performance by analyzing sales, profitability, customer purchasing patterns, product categories, and regional performance.

The analysis involves data cleaning, data transformation, exploratory data analysis (EDA), KPI development, and business intelligence reporting using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

Through interactive visualizations and structured analysis, the project explores key business metrics, identifies sales trends, evaluates profit performance, and highlights areas of improvement. It focuses on transforming raw retail data into meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions.

The project demonstrates practical skills in data preparation, analytical problem-solving, business performance evaluation, DAX calculations, dashboard development, and data visualization.

## Tech Stack

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data Cleaning, KPI Summary, PivotTables, and Pivot Charts.
- **Power BI:** DAX Measures, Data Modeling, and Interactive Data Visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data Transformation and Preparation.
- **DAX:** KPI Calculations and Business Metrics Analysis.

## Data Source

The dataset used in this project is the **Sample Superstore Dataset**, sourced from Kaggle. It contains retail transaction data, including sales, profit, discounts, product categories, customer details, and regional information.
## Key Highlights & Business Insights

- **Overall Business Performance:** The business generated approximately $2.297M in total sales and $286.40K in total profit, with an overall profit margin of 12.47%.

- **Order & Customer Analysis:** The dashboard represents approximately 5K orders and 793 unique customers, providing an overview of business reach and transaction activity.

- **Category Performance:** Technology contributes the highest sales among the three product categories, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

- **Customer Segment Analysis:** The Consumer segment accounts for the largest share of sales (approximately 50%), followed by Corporate and Home Office.

- **Sub-Category Performance:** Phones and Chairs are among the leading sub-categories in terms of sales, highlighting their significant contribution to overall revenue.

- **Sales Trend Analysis:** Monthly sales and profit show fluctuations throughout the year, with noticeable peaks and variations in business performance.

- **Geographical Analysis:** The dashboard provides a state-wise and regional view of sales performance, helping identify geographical patterns in retail business activity.

- **Profitability Analysis:** Comparing sales and profit across product categories helps evaluate revenue contribution and understand differences in category-level financial performance.
